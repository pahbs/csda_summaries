What the vendor terms mean

The vendors name the same things differently. "TOA" is not the same on both: Airbus pixels are already reflectance, Vantor pixels still need converting.

AspectIn plain wordsAirbus calls itVantor Legion calls it
GeometryTerrain-corrected and map-projected: the one to use for overlays and comparing datesORT (ORTHO)LV3D (Ortho, fine DEM)
GeometryMap-projected with a coarse DEM only, so terrain can still shift featuresnot deliveredLV2A (Standard, coarse DEM)
GeometryAs the sensor saw it, not map-projectedSEN (SENSOR)not delivered
BandsPanchromatic: one band, the sharpestP, PANP2AS, P3DS
BandsMultispectralMS: 4 bands. MS-FS (Pléiades Neo): 6 bands, adding deep blue and red edgeM2AS, M3DS: 8 bands (coastal, blue, green, yellow, red, 2 red edge, NIR)
BandsPansharpened: multispectral at the pan pixel sizenot deliveredS3DS: usually 3 bands (RGB)
RadiometryTop of atmosphereRFL (REFLECTANCE): pixels are already reflectanceEnhancement Off: pixels are calibrated DN, converted to radiance and reflectance with absCalFactor
RadiometrySurface reflectanceSR (SURFACE_REFLECTANCE)ACOMP (atmospheric compensation), read as surface reflectance: the metadata do not say so, absCalFactor is −999 and pixel values are consistent with reflectance × 10,000
RadiometryRaw counts, with gains in the metadata to reach TOA radianceBAS (BASIC)not delivered

Notes

  • Acqs = acquisitions in CSDA STAC at the site. New = not there at the last run. On ADAPT = at least one of its products is on ADAPT. Other = no TOA or SR product known.
  • Acquisition = one satellite pass (Vantor catalog ID). Products = pan and MS products; an acquisition delivered with both radiometric versions has four.
  • CSDA STAC shows both radiometric versions as the same level, so radiometry is read from the .IMD and is known only for products on ADAPT.
  • A Legion item ID ends in the product code: 200014660685_01_P001_M2AS is multispectral (M), level 2A.
