Candidate Acquisition Thumbnail Viewer

A tool supporting manual SME thumbnail review of image quality & final request compilation prior to sending to Data Management Team for vendor data purchase.

Keys: K keep, D drop, U undo, ←/→ move, 1–6 drop with a reason, Enter open on the map, Esc close. Save review writes the kept acquisitions (CSV) and the review (JSON) next to the selection.
