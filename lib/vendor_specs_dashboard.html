Image footprint vs. detail

Multispectral GSD at nadir (x) against swath width (y), both log scales. Dashed lines count the pixels across the swath (swath ÷ GSD): most sensors sit between about 3,000 and 30,000, so finer detail comes with a narrower footprint. Hover a point for its specs.

Key specs side by side

One row per constellation, sorted by multispectral GSD; each panel has its own log axis. Filled dot = multispectral GSD, open dot = panchromatic GSD. Hover a row.

Relative spectral response curves

One panel per constellation on a shared wavelength axis; each line is one band of one sensor, colored by band. Hover a curve for its sensor, band and response. Click a band in the key to hide or show it. To compare a few, list them in Find, separated by commas.

Band centers and widths

For the constellations and bands shown above. Center = response-weighted mean wavelength; FWHM = width at half the peak response. Where a constellation has several sensors, the center is a range and the FWHM a median.

Cross-track swaths with evaluation AOIs

Every constellation's swath as a square at true ground scale, centered on the chosen evaluation AOI, with the AOI shaded behind them (square side = swath width; a nominal scene; pushbroom strips can be much longer). Hover a square or a name for its specs; click a name to zoom to it.

Launches, orbits and satellite size

One mark per sensor row in the sheet, at its launch date (x) and orbit altitude (y). Marker shape = orbit type, size = satellite size class, color = data owner or sector (key above); open = planned. The line runs to the end date, or to today while in operation. Hover a mark for details.
